## Suggested announcement text

For the ResearchGate update field, or LinkedIn:

> New preprint. IAB privacy signaling (GPP, TCF, US Privacy, OpenRTB regs)
> is built to carry consumer choice across the bid. I asked how much of
> that stack a receiver can actually check, and what a check decides.
>
> On the GPP string spec, 61.5% of conformance sentences are shape and
> 76.9% decide nothing about a person. On the TCF document as published,
> 67.4% are process a bid receiver cannot observe. Combined, a green GPP
> check covers about a quarter of the portfolio and is not consent, not
> COPPA, and not a bound on automatic content recognition.
>
> The web control, from six captures of US header bidding, shows the
> syntax layer itself is stale: leftover regs.ext.gdpr on 38 of 79 sites,
> deprecated US Privacy still in ext, GPP sometimes missing its gpp_sid,
> and zero TCF Europe sections in 20,226 requests. Connected TV is the
> stress test for meaning: the CMP GPP assumes is often missing, the
> stitcher is the declarer of record, and ACR never reads those fields.
>
> Artifacts at github.com/aleksUIX/iab-privacy-checkability. Every number
> in the paper is produced by a script.
